Day 1 Details

Spiritual and Cultural Highlights


  1. ISKCON Temple: Special for their dedication to Lord Krishna and Radha.

    2. *No Entry Fee

  2. Bull Temple: Its massive, monolithic statue of Nandi, the sacred bull of Lord Shiva.

    3. *No Entry Fee

  3. Visvesvaraya Museum: This splendid museum, dedicated to the memory of Bharat Ratna M. Visvesvaraya.

    4. *₹100 per person (Museum)

  4. Ranga Shankara Theatre: Bengaluru is renowned as a prominent venue for performing arts.

    5. *₹200 to ₹400 per person

  5. Church street: A vibrant tourist hotspot, a major shopping and nightlife area, and its lively atmosphere.

    6. *No Fee

Day 2 Details

Iconic Landmarks


  1. Nandhi hills: Scenic beauty, pleasant weather, and historical significance.

    2. *₹30 per person

  2. Isha Foundation: Its Adiyogi statue, a 112-foot tall representation of Lord Shiva.

    3. *₹100 to ₹200 per person(Light Show)

  3. Lalbagh Botanical Gardens: Its vast collection of diverse plant species.

    4. *₹25 per person

  4. Bangalore Palace: Famous for its magnificent Tudor and Scottish Gothic architecture.

    5. *₹230 per person

  5. MG Road & Brigade Road: Lively atmosphere, diverse shopping options.

    6. *No Entry Fee

Day 3 Details

Trip to Mysore



  1. Mysore Palace: Its stunning Indo-Saracenic architecture.

    2. *₹100 per person

  2. Chamundeshwari Temple: A prominent Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari.

    3. *No entry fee

  3. St. Philomena's Church: Famous for its Neo-Gothic architecture.

    4. *No entry fee

Day 4 Details

Nature and Adventure


  1. Bannerghatta National Park: Its safaris, particularly the lion and tiger safaris, along with a zoo, butterfly park, and nature camp.

    2. *₹100 per person

  2. Butterfly Park: India's first butterfly park.

    3. *₹50 per person

  3. Cubbon Park: Lush greenery, historical buildings like the State Central Library.

    4. *No entry fee

  4. Vidhana Soudha: The seat of the Karnataka state legislature.

    5. *Entry restricted
