Cochin Start


  1. Fort Kochi Walk: Famous for its rich history, landmarks, and seaside promenade.

    2. *No Entry Fee

  2. Indo-Portuguese Museum: Its collection of artifacts that showcase the Portuguese influence on the region's art, culture, and history.

    3. *₹10 per person

    4. *No Entry Fee

Munnar Heights



  1. Tea Garden Visit: The chance to see how tea is grown and processed, and for the beauty of the tea plantations.

    2. *₹75 per person

  2. Eravikulam National Park: Biodiversity, stunning landscapes, and being home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr.
