Day 1 Details

Explore beautiful beaches and vibrant culture of North Goa.


  1. Anjuna Beach: Famous for its nightlife and flea market.

    2. *No entry fee

  2. Chapora Fort: Offers stunning views of the coastline.

    3. *No entry fee

  3. Sweet Lake: A serene spot for relaxation.

    4. *No entry fee

  4. Arambol Beach: Known for its bohemian vibe and live music.

    5. *No entry fee

Day 2 Details

Experience the lively atmosphere of North Goa.


  1. Baga Beach: Popular for water sports and nightlife.

    2. *No entry fee

  2. Para Road: Scenic drive with beautiful views.

    3. *No entry fee

  3. Aguada Fort: Historical site with panoramic views.

    4. *No entry fee

  4. Boating in Coco Jetty: Enjoy the mangrove forests.

    5. *The cost of a boat trip starts from Rs 1499

Day 3 Details

Engage in various activities like water sports, sightseeing, and local tours.



  1. Parasailing: Parasailing is a recreational activity where you are pulled behind a boat or vehicle while wearing a parachute.

    2. *Price:- ₹800 - ₹1,200 per person


  2. Jet Skiing: The activity of using a Jet Ski to travel across water.

    3. *Price:- ₹500 - ₹800 per person




  3. Scuba Diving: Scuba diving is a mode of underwater diving whereby divers use breathing equipment.

    4. *Price:- ₹3,000 - ₹5,000 per person



  4. Flyboarding: Flyboarding is a water sport where a rider stands on a board attached to a watercraft by a hose.

    5. *Price:- ₹2,500 - ₹3,500 per person


  5. Wind Surfing: Windsurfing is a wind-propelled water sport that is a combination of sailing and surfing.

    6. *Price:- ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 per person
